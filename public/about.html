About Midnight Nachos

What this site is

Midnight Nachos is a late-night recipe site. It has a collection of original snack and drink recipes sorted by how much effort you have left, a set of kitchen guides, an AI recipe helper that suggests something from the ingredients you have, and a wall where readers share their own late-night food stories.

The recipes and guides

Every recipe in the recipe collection is our own. The kitchen guides cover technique and food safety, and cite official guidance where safety is involved.

The shop

The shop lists print-on-demand apparel and links to products on Amazon. Midnight Nachos does not sell food.

Who is behind it

Midnight Nachos is published by Mike Hamilton, an independent producer and publisher based in Texas. Original pieces are published under the Midnight Nachos Kitchen byline. How they are written and checked is set out in our editorial policy.

How the site is funded

The site earns money from advertising, from print-on-demand merchandise, and from affiliate links to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.
