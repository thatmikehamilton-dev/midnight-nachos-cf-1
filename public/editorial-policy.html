Editorial Policy

What we publish

We publish original recipes and practical kitchen guides. Reader stories are written by readers and are labeled as such.

How pieces are written and checked

Drafts are prepared with the help of AI writing tools and reviewed before publication. We stick to facts that can be confirmed in the sources listed at the end of each piece, and we say so plainly when something is opinion, disputed or uncertain. The explanations, comparisons and recommendations are our own.

Other people’s work

Reader stories belong to the people who wrote them. Recipes suggested by the AI recipe helper are generated on request and have not been tested by us; treat them as ideas, and use normal kitchen judgment. If you want something of yours removed, contact us and we will take it down.

Corrections and updates

When we get something wrong, we fix the page and note the date it changed. To report an error, use the contact page and include the page address and, if you can, a source we can check.

Advertising and paid links

Affiliate links are identified in the shop. A commission never decides which recipe or technique we recommend.
