Privacy Policy

Summary

Effective November 16, 2026. This policy covers midnightnachos.com. We collect very little. Advertising partners, including Google, may use cookies as described below.

Information we collect

  • Stories you post. If you submit a story, the text and the name you give are stored and shown publicly on the site.
  • AI recipe helper. What you type into the recipe helper is sent to an AI service to generate a reply. Do not include personal information.
  • Email you send us. If you write to us, we keep the message so we can reply.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. More detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Links to other sites

The shop links to Amazon and to a print-on-demand merchandise provider. When you follow a link, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

Service providers

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes requests in order to deliver pages and protect the site. The AI recipe helper uses a third-party AI provider to generate replies.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, opt out of personalized ads using the links above, and unsubscribe from any email we send. To ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted, write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change with it. Questions: thatmikehamilton@gmail.com or the contact page.
