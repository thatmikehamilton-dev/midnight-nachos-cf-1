Cinnamon Toast Crunch Shake

The sweet-tooth savior. Thick, creamy vanilla milk blended with crunchy cinnamon sugar cereal, creating the ultimate cinnamilk-infused milkshake.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Easy (No Heat) · About 3 mins

Ingredients

  • 3 scoops Vanilla Bean Ice Cream
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • 3/4 cup Cinnamon Toast Crunch cereal (split into 1/2 cup for blending, 1/4 cup for topping)
  • 1/2 tsp Ground Cinnamon
  • Whipped Cream & Caramel sauce (optional but highly recommended)

Method

  1. Throw the ice cream, milk, half cup of cereal, and ground cinnamon into a blender.
  2. Pulse until smooth but still featuring tiny micro-crunches of cinnamon sugar goodness.
  3. Pour into a tall glass.
  4. Top with a mountain of whipped cream, a heavy swirl of caramel sauce, and the remaining whole cereal pieces.
  5. Grab a straw and a spoon. Sip slowly while watching lofi animations.
