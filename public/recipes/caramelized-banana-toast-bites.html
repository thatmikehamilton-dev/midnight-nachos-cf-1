Caramelized Banana Toast Bites

Crispy pan-seared brioche bread cubes tossed in cinnamon sugar, topped with hot bananas caramelized in butter and brown sugar.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Medium (Stove/Oven) · About 8 mins

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of thick Brioche bread (cut into cubes)
  • 1 firm Banana (sliced into rounds)
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 2 tbsp Brown Sugar
  • 1/2 tsp Ground Cinnamon

Method

  1. Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add the brioche bread cubes and toast, stirring frequently, until crispy and golden. Remove to a bowl and toss with cinnamon sugar.
  3. In the same hot skillet, melt the remaining butter and add the brown sugar, creating a bubbling syrup.
  4. Add banana slices. Let cook undisturbed for 2 minutes to caramelize, then flip and cook for 1 more minute.
  5. Pour the hot, sticky caramelized bananas over the crispy toast bites and eat with a toothpick.
