Slushy Mountain Dew Freeze

A neon green slushy beverage made by blending frozen Gatorade cubes with cold Mountain Dew. Pure ice-cold energy.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Easy (No Heat) · About 3 mins

Ingredients

  • 10-12 frozen Blue or Red Gatorade ice cubes
  • 1/2 can cold Mountain Dew
  • 1 tbsp Fresh Lime juice
  • Sour gummy worms (optional)

Method

  1. Throw your pre-frozen Gatorade ice cubes into a blender.
  2. Add the lime juice and Mountain Dew.
  3. Blend on high speed until a snowy, smooth, slushy consistency forms.
  4. Pour into a tall glass, garnish with sour gummy worms, and drink immediately.
