Pan-Fried Oreos (Fair Ground Classic)

The absolute peak of carnival sweetness. Oreos dipped in thick pancake batter and shallow-fried in a skillet until puffy, golden, and melting inside.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · High Effort (Culinary Master) · About 15 mins

Ingredients

  • 10 double-stuffed Oreo cookies
  • 1 cup dry Pancake mix
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 cup Vegetable oil (for shallow frying)
  • Heaps of powdered sugar

Method

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the pancake mix, milk, and egg until a smooth, thick batter forms.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a deep skillet over medium-high heat (350°F / 175°C). Ensure it's hot by dropping a bit of batter in (it should sizzle instantly).
  3. Drop an Oreo into the batter, turning to coat it completely.
  4. Carefully lower the coated cookie into the hot oil. Repeat with 4-5 cookies at a time.
  5. Fry for 1.5 to 2 minutes per side until puffy and golden brown.
  6. Remove to a paper-towel-lined plate. Dust immediately with a snowstorm of powdered sugar.
