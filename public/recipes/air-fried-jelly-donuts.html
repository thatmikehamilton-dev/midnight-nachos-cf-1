Air Fried Jelly Donuts

Golden, crispy, cinnamon-sugar crusted donut bites made with simple ingredients. Stuffed with hot, sweet strawberry jelly.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Medium (Stove/Oven) · About 7 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 can of refrigerated Biscuit dough
  • 1/2 cup Strawberry Jelly or Jam
  • 3 tbsp Melted Butter
  • 1/2 cup Cinnamon Sugar
  • Powdered sugar

Method

  1. Preheat air fryer to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cut biscuit rounds into quarters. Roll each quarter into a neat ball.
  3. Air fry the dough balls for 5-6 minutes until puffed up and golden brown.
  4. Brush the hot donut holes with melted butter and roll them in cinnamon sugar.
  5. Using a skewer or chopstick, poke a hole in each donut. Use a piping bag or small spoon to squeeze a dollop of strawberry jelly into the center.
  6. Dust with powdered sugar and eat warm.
