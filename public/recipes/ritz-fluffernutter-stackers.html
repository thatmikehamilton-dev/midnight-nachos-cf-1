Ritz FlufferNutter Stackers

Buttry, salty Ritz crackers stacked high with sweet peanut butter, fluffy marshmallow fluff, and sliced bananas. The ultimate instant snack.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Easy (No Heat) · About 3 mins

Ingredients

  • 10 Ritz Crackers
  • 3 tbsp Creamy Peanut Butter
  • 3 tbsp Marshmallow Fluff
  • 1/2 Banana (sliced)

Method

  1. Lay out 10 Ritz crackers on a plate.
  2. Spread peanut butter on 5 crackers and marshmallow fluff on the other 5.
  3. Place a thin banana slice on top of the peanut butter crackers.
  4. Sandwich the fluff crackers and peanut butter crackers together. Squish gently and devour.
