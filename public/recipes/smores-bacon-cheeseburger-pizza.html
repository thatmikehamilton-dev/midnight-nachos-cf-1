S'mores Bacon Cheeseburger Pizza

An unbelievable sweet-savory culinary fusion. Pizza dough topped with ground beef, cheddar, crispy bacon, then layered with mini marshmallows and chocolate chips.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · High Effort (Culinary Master) · About 28 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 pre-made Pizza crust
  • 1/2 cup Marinara sauce
  • 1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup cooked Ground Beef
  • 1/4 cup cooked crispy Bacon bits
  • 1/3 cup Chocolate Chips
  • 1/2 cup Mini Marshmallows

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Spread marinara, cheddar, ground beef, and bacon across the pizza crust.
  3. Bake for 10-12 minutes until the cheese is melted and crust is golden.
  4. Pull the hot pizza out of the oven, then instantly scatter the chocolate chips and mini marshmallows over the top.
  5. Return to the oven for 2 minutes on Broil until the marshmallows are toasted golden brown.
