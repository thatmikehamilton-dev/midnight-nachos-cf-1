Fresh Raspberry Lime Caviar Soda

A highly scientific and beautiful late-night molecular mocktail. Sweet sparkling lime soda featuring popping spheres of homemade raspberry juice caviar.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · High Effort (Culinary Master) · About 20 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh Raspberries
  • 1/2 cup Water & 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 packet Gelatin or Agar Agar
  • 1 cup cold Vegetable Oil (chilled in freezer for 2 hours)
  • 1 bottle Sprite or Lime Sparkling Water

Method

  1. Simmer raspberries, water, and sugar in a pan. Strain to get pure raspberry syrup. Stir in gelatin.
  2. Fill a dropper or syringe with the warm raspberry mixture.
  3. Gently drip the raspberry syrup into the ice-cold vegetable oil. The drops will instantly form perfect spheres and sink.
  4. Strain the spheres out of the oil and rinse with cold water.
  5. Spoon your fresh raspberry caviar into a glass, pour ice-cold Sprite over it, and watch the crimson pearls dance.
