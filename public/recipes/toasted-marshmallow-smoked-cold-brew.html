Toasted Marshmallow Smoked Cold Brew

A gorgeous, sweet, caffeinated dessert shake. Cold brew coffee blended with real oven-toasted marshmallows, vanilla bean ice cream, and finished with a burnt marshmallow skewer.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · High Effort (Culinary Master) · About 15 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 cup strong Cold Brew coffee
  • 6 large Marshmallows
  • 3 scoops Vanilla Ice cream
  • 2 tbsp Chocolate Syrup

Method

  1. Arrange the marshmallows on a foil-lined sheet. Broil in the oven for 2 minutes, watching closely, until they are puffed and deeply blackened/toasted.
  2. Scrape 4 of the hot, sticky toasted marshmallows directly into a blender.
  3. Add cold brew, vanilla ice cream, and chocolate syrup. Blend on high until completely incorporated.
  4. Pour into a glass rimmed with chocolate syrup, and garnish with the remaining two toasted marshmallows on a toothpick.
