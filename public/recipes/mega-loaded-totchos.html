Mega-Loaded Totchos

Tater tots replace chips in this ultimate loaded oven-baked masterpiece. Crispy, golden, covered in bubbling cheese and your favorite toppings.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · High Effort (Culinary Master) · About 25 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 bag of Frozen Tater Tots
  • 1 cup Shredded Cheddar & Jack cheese
  • 1/4 cup Jalapeño slices (pickled)
  • 1/3 cup black beans or ground beef
  • 2 tbsp Sour Cream
  • 1/2 cup Guacamole

Method

  1. Bake the tater tots on a baking sheet at 425°F (220°C) for 20 minutes until they are ultra-crisp.
  2. Push the cooked tots together into a single tight layer, then sprinkle with beans/beef, jalapeños, and cheese.
  3. Return to the oven for 4-5 minutes until the cheese is fully melted and bubbling.
  4. Carefully transfer to a serving platter and top with cold sour cream and guacamole dollops.
