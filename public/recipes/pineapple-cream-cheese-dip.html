Pineapple Cream Cheese Dip

An unexpected tropical, sweet-savory flavor bomb. Smooth, cold cream cheese topped with sweet crushed pineapple and a heavy drizzle of sriracha.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Easy (No Heat) · About 3 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 block (8 oz) Cream Cheese
  • 1/2 cup crushed canned Pineapple (drained)
  • 2 tbsp Sriracha or Cholula hot sauce
  • 1 bag of Tortilla Chips

Method

  1. Place the cream cheese block flat on a serving platter.
  2. Spoon the drained crushed pineapple evenly over the top of the cheese block.
  3. Drizzle sriracha over the pineapple layer in an elegant zig-zag.
  4. Scoop with tortilla chips, ensuring you get all three layers in one bite.
