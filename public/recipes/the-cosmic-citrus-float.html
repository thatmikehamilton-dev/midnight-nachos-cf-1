The Cosmic Citrus Float

A fizzy, refreshing beverage that cuts through any late-night dry mouth. The vanilla ice cream melts into orange soda, creating a magical creamsicle nebula.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Easy (No Heat) · About 2 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 can Orange Soda (or Mountain Dew for a wilder ride)
  • 2 scoops Vanilla Bean Ice Cream
  • A splash of lime juice
  • 1 slice of fresh Orange or lime for garnish

Method

  1. Grab a large frosted mug.
  2. Add a small splash of lime juice to the bottom, then drop in the scoops of vanilla ice cream.
  3. Slowly pour the orange soda over the ice cream, letting it foam up to the brim.
  4. Garnish with an orange slice.
  5. Enjoy the fizzy, creamy cosmic sunset in your cup.
