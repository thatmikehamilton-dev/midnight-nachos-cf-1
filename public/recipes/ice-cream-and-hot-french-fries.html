Ice Cream & Hot French Fries

The absolute pinnacle of sweet-salty-hot-cold contrast. Hot, crispy salted french fries dipped in velvety sweet vanilla bean ice cream.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Easy (No Heat) · About 3 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 plate of hot, crispy Salted French Fries
  • 2 scoops premium Vanilla Bean Ice Cream
  • A light sprinkle of sea salt

Method

  1. Prepare your fries so they are extremely crispy and salted immediately while hot.
  2. Scoop cold vanilla ice cream into a small dipping bowl.
  3. Grab a hot fry, dip it deep into the cold sweet ice cream, and eat. Feel the flavor dimensions collide.
