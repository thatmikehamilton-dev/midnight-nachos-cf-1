The Crunchwrap Supreme Hybrid

The ultimate late-night combination. An outer crispy flour tortilla wrapped around seasoned beef, gooey cheddar cheese, spicy jalapeno ranch, and a hidden center layer of crunchy Doritos.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Medium (Stove/Oven) · About 8 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 large Flour Tortilla
  • 1 small corn tostada shell (or a handful of Nacho Cheese Doritos)
  • 1/2 cup Shredded Cheddar or Nacho Cheese sauce
  • 1/3 cup seasoned Ground Beef or black beans
  • Shredded lettuce and sour cream
  • 1 tsp butter (for grilling)

Method

  1. Warm the large flour tortilla in the microwave for 10 seconds to make it super stretchy.
  2. Lay it flat and place your warm seasoned beef or beans in the center, followed by a heavy pour of nacho cheese sauce.
  3. Place your Doritos or tostada shell directly on top of the cheese layer.
  4. Add the lettuce, sour cream, and a splash of hot sauce.
  5. Fold the edges of the flour tortilla up and over the center, pleating it until it is fully sealed in a hexagon shape.
  6. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat. Place the wrap seam-side down first. Grill for 3 minutes until golden brown, then flip and grill the other side.
  7. Let cool for 1 minute so you don't burn your tongue, then slice in half and enjoy.
