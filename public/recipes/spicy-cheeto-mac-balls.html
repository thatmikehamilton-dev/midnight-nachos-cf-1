Spicy Cheeto Mac Balls

Deep-fried, crispy spheres of creamy mac and cheese coated in a fiery red crust of crushed Flamin' Hot Cheetos.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · High Effort (Culinary Master) · About 25 mins

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cold, thick Mac & Cheese
  • 2 cups Flamin' Hot Cheetos (ground to fine crumbs)
  • 2 Eggs (beaten)
  • 1/2 cup Flour
  • Oil for deep frying

Method

  1. Scoop the cold mac & cheese and roll into tight 2-inch balls. Freeze on a sheet for 15 minutes to firm up.
  2. Set up three bowls: one with flour, one with beaten eggs, and one with the crushed Hot Cheeto crumbs.
  3. Roll each frozen mac ball in flour, then dip in egg, then coat heavily in Cheeto crumbs.
  4. Deep fry in hot oil (350°F / 175°C) for 3-4 minutes until the outer crust is crispy and the inside is piping hot.
  5. Serve with ranch dressing.
