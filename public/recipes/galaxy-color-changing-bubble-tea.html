Galaxy Color-Changing Bubble Tea

An incredibly visual, magical tea. Butterfly pea flower tea creates a deep blue liquid that turns pastel pink/purple when fresh lime juice is introduced over sweet tapioca pearls.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · High Effort (Culinary Master) · About 25 mins

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp dried Butterfly Pea Flowers
  • 1/2 cup warm Tapioca pearls (Boba)
  • 1 tbsp Honey or Brown Sugar syrup
  • 1/2 cup Milk or coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp fresh Lime juice

Method

  1. Steep the butterfly pea flowers in 1 cup of hot water for 5 minutes. Strain to get a royal blue tea, then let cool.
  2. In a serving glass, add the warm tapioca pearls and coat in brown sugar syrup.
  3. Add crushed ice to the top of the glass, then pour in the milk.
  4. Slowly pour in the blue butterfly pea tea, creating a gorgeous layered blue/white drink.
  5. Right before sipping, squeeze the fresh lime juice over the top. Watch the acidic citrus turn the blue tea into a sparkling violet purple galaxy.
