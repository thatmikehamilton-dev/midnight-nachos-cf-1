Ramen Carbonara Upgrade

How to make a $0.50 instant ramen pack taste like a $20 Italian meal in less than five minutes. Rich, creamy, and loaded with savory cheese.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Medium (Stove/Oven) · About 5 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 pack Instant Ramen (Chicken or Tonkotsu flavor)
  • 1 fresh Egg Yolk
  • 2 tbsp Grated Parmesan Cheese
  • 1 tbsp Butter
  • 1 clove Garlic (minced, or garlic powder if lazy)
  • Crushed bacon bits or green onions

Method

  1. Boil the ramen noodles in water for 3 minutes. Drain, but reserve 1/4 cup of the hot noodle water.
  2. While the noodles are boiling, whisk the egg yolk, grated parmesan cheese, and half of the ramen seasoning packet together in a small bowl until it forms a thick paste.
  3. In your hot empty pot, melt the butter over low heat and quickly saute the minced garlic for 30 seconds.
  4. Dump the hot noodles into the pot, turn off the heat completely (crucial step, or the egg will scramble!).
  5. Pour in the egg-cheese paste and the reserved hot noodle water. Toss rapidly with tongs or a fork. The heat of the noodles and water will cook the egg yolk into a thick, glossy, luxurious sauce.
  6. Top with bacon bits, green onions, and extra black pepper.
