Chai Tea White Hot Chocolate

A fragrant, luxurious late-night tea hybrid. Creamy milk simmered with white chocolate chips and an authentic chai spice blend.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Medium (Stove/Oven) · About 5 mins

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups Whole Milk
  • 1/3 cup White Chocolate chips
  • 1 Chai Tea bag
  • 1/4 tsp Vanilla extract
  • A pinch of ground nutmeg

Method

  1. Pour milk into a saucepan and add the chai tea bag. Simmer over low heat for 3 minutes to steep.
  2. Remove the tea bag, squeezing out the herbal juices.
  3. Add white chocolate chips and vanilla, stirring constantly until the chocolate melts into a velvety white hot cocoa.
  4. Pour into a mug and top with nutmeg.
