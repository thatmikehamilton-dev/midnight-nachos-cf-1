Blueberry Dream Sleepy Milk

A soothing, pastel-purple hot beverage designed to ease you into sleep. Warm milk infused with fresh simmered blueberry syrup and vanilla.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Medium (Stove/Oven) · About 5 mins

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups Milk (Oat milk or Whole milk work great)
  • 1/3 cup Blueberries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tbsp Honey or Maple Syrup
  • 1/2 tsp Vanilla extract
  • A tiny pinch of cinnamon

Method

  1. In a small saucepan, combine blueberries, honey, vanilla, and cinnamon over medium heat. Simmer and smash with a fork for 2-3 minutes until jammy.
  2. Strain the purple blueberry juice/syrup into a mug.
  3. In the same saucepan, warm your milk over low heat until hot and steaming.
  4. Pour the hot milk into your mug, stirring it into the blueberry syrup to create a gorgeous purple sleep aid.
