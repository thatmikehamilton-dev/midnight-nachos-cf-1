Crispy Tortilla Pizza

Super crispy thin-crust pizza made in minutes using a simple flour tortilla. Perfect for scratching that fast-food pizza itch.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Medium (Stove/Oven) · About 5 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 large Flour Tortilla
  • 1/4 cup Marinara or pizza sauce
  • 1/2 cup Shredded Mozzarella cheese
  • 12 slices of Pepperoni
  • A sprinkle of Italian seasoning & red pepper flakes

Method

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) or prepare a large skillet on medium heat.
  2. If using a skillet, toast one side of the tortilla for 1 minute, then flip it.
  3. Spread the pizza sauce evenly to the very edges of the tortilla.
  4. Scatter mozzarella and pepperonis across the sauce.
  5. Cover the skillet with a lid and cook for 3-4 minutes until the cheese is bubbling and the tortilla is extremely crispy on the bottom.
  6. Slice into wedges and sprinkle with red pepper flakes.
