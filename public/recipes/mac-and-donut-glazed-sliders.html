Mac & Donut Glazed Sliders

Creamy mac and cheese stuffed between a sliced glazed donut, crusted in butter and pan-fried like a sweet-savory grilled cheese.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · High Effort (Culinary Master) · About 18 mins

Ingredients

  • 2 whole Glazed Donuts (sliced in half horizontally)
  • 1 cup leftover Mac & Cheese
  • 4 slices of Cheddar Cheese
  • 2 tbsp Butter

Method

  1. In a small pan, reheat your mac & cheese until warm and sticky.
  2. Open your sliced glazed donuts. Lay a slice of cheddar cheese on the bottom half of each donut.
  3. Spoon a massive pile of warm mac & cheese on top of the cheese slices.
  4. Add the second slice of cheddar cheese on top of the mac layer, then close the donuts.
  5. Melt butter in a skillet over low heat. Fry the donuts, flipping carefully, until the glaze caramelizes and the cheese melts completely.
