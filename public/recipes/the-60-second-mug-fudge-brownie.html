The 60-Second Mug Fudge Brownie

The emergency sweet-tooth solution. A rich, gooey, molten chocolate fudge brownie cooked right in your favorite mug in under a minute.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Easy (No Heat) · About 1 min

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp All-Purpose Flour
  • 2 tbsp Sugar
  • 1 tbsp Cocoa Powder
  • 1 tbsp Melted Butter (or oil)
  • 2 tbsp Milk
  • 1 tbsp Chocolate Chips

Method

  1. In a microwave-safe mug, whisk together the flour, sugar, and cocoa powder with a fork.
  2. Add the milk and melted butter, stirring until a smooth, thick chocolate batter forms.
  3. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Microwave on High for 45 to 50 seconds. The center should still look slightly wet and molten.
  5. Let cool for 2 minutes (it will be extremely hot!). Top with vanilla ice cream if desired.
