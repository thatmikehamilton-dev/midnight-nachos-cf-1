The S'mores Quesadilla

A campfire classic adapted for indoor late-night cravings. Crispy toasted flour tortilla filled with melted chocolate and toasted marshmallows.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Medium (Stove/Oven) · About 5 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 large Flour Tortilla
  • 1/2 cup Mini Marshmallows
  • 1/3 cup Chocolate Chips
  • 2 Graham Crackers (crushed)
  • 1 tsp Butter

Method

  1. Lay the tortilla flat. Sprinkle chocolate chips and crushed graham crackers on one half.
  2. Pile the mini marshmallows on top of the chocolate.
  3. Fold the empty half of the tortilla over the filling to create a half-moon.
  4. Melt butter in a skillet over medium-low heat. Place the fold-over inside.
  5. Grill for 2-3 minutes per side, pressing down with a spatula, until the chocolate is gooey and marshmallows are fully melted.
