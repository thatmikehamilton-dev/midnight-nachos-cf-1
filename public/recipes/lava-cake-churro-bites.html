Lava Cake Churro Bites

Deep, chocolatey, and crisp. Hollow churro-like pastry pockets made with cocoa dough, fried, and injected with molten chocolate hazelnut cream.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · High Effort (Culinary Master) · About 20 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Water
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 1 cup Flour
  • 2 tbsp Cocoa Powder
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1/2 cup Nutella (for stuffing)
  • Cinnamon sugar

Method

  1. Boil water and butter in a saucepan. Stir in flour and cocoa powder rapidly until a ball forms.
  2. Remove from heat, let cool slightly, then beat in eggs one at a time until smooth.
  3. Load the dough into a pastry bag with a star tip.
  4. Pipe 2-inch chunks of dough into hot vegetable oil. Fry for 4 minutes until crispy.
  5. Roll the hot bites in cinnamon sugar.
  6. Use a piping bag with a thin tip to inject Nutella directly into the center of each hot bite.
