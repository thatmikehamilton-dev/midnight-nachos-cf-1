Double-Decker Nacho Crackers

Cold savory snacks constructed to perfection. Cheesy crackers stacked high with cream cheese, sliced turkey, cheddar squares, and a touch of sweet honey-mustard.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Easy (No Heat) · About 3 mins

Ingredients

  • 10 Ritz or Club crackers
  • 4 tbsp cream cheese
  • 2 slices deli Turkey breast (torn into small pieces)
  • 5 small squares of Cheddar cheese
  • Honey mustard drizzle

Method

  1. Lay out 5 crackers as your foundation.
  2. Spread cream cheese over each, then press a piece of deli turkey on top.
  3. Add a drizzle of honey mustard and a square of cheddar cheese.
  4. Place the second cracker on top, squishing down gently until the cream cheese acts as adhesive.
  5. Pop them in your mouth in one single bite.
