Triple-Thick Cheeseburger Quesadilla

Two quesadillas serving as the top and bottom buns of a giant, juicy, cheese-stuffed double burger patty. Epic proportions.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · High Effort (Culinary Master) · About 22 mins

Ingredients

  • 4 medium Flour Tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded Cheddar & Jack cheese
  • 1/2 lb Ground Beef
  • 1/4 cup chopped pickles & onions
  • 4 tbsp Secret Sauce (Mayo, Ketchup, Relish)

Method

  1. Season and cook two flat ground beef patties in a hot skillet until cooked through and seared.
  2. In a separate pan, make 2 simple cheese quesadillas using the 4 tortillas and shredded cheese.
  3. Lay down the first cheese quesadilla on a cutting board.
  4. Spread Secret Sauce over it, then layer pickles, onions, and the double burger patties.
  5. Top with the second warm cheese quesadilla.
  6. Press down firmly, slice into quarters, and experience burger nirvana.
