Spicy Mango Michelada Mocktail

Sweet, sour, spicy, and deeply refreshing. Thick mango nectar combined with lime, ginger beer, and a heavy Tajin chili rim.

By Midnight Nachos Kitchen · Easy (No Heat) · About 4 mins

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Mango Nectar
  • 1 bottle cold Ginger Beer
  • 2 tbsp fresh Lime juice
  • 1 tbsp Chamoy sauce & 2 tbsp Tajín seasoning (for rimming)

Method

  1. Rim a tall glass with chamoy, then dip into Tajin chili seasoning.
  2. Fill the glass with crushed ice.
  3. Pour in the lime juice and mango nectar.
  4. Top off with ice-cold ginger beer, stir gently, and sip the sweet-and-spicy masterpiece.
